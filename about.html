About Us

Free PDF tools that respect your privacy.

Why we built this site

Most online PDF tools work the same way: you upload your file to their server, they process it, and you download the result. That is fine for a flyer or a recipe. It is not fine for a passport scan, a bank statement, a signed contract, or a medical form.

Once a file is on someone else’s server, you cannot see where it goes, who can access it, or how long it is kept. We did not like that trade-off, so we built pdfimageconverter.online to avoid it.

How our tools work

Every tool on this site runs inside your web browser, on your own device. Your files are opened, processed, and turned into a new file right there. They are never sent to our servers, because our servers never receive them. Behind the scenes, the tools use well-known open-source libraries: jsPDF for creating PDFs, pdf-lib for merging them, pdf.js for reading PDF text, and SheetJS for reading spreadsheets.

You can check this yourself. Open your browser’s developer tools, go to the Network tab, and use any tool. You will see the page code load, but you will not see your files being uploaded.

What we offer

  • Image to PDF turns JPG, PNG, and WEBP images into one PDF.
  • Merge PDF combines several PDF files into one.
  • Excel to PDF converts spreadsheets and CSV files into a clean PDF table.
  • AI PDF Summarizer writes a short summary of a PDF using the AI built into desktop Chrome, so the text stays on your device.

We also write plain-English guides on our blog to help with everyday PDF problems.

Being honest about limits

Browser-based tools have trade-offs, and we would rather tell you than hide them. Very large files can be slow on older devices, because your own device does the work. The Excel tool makes a simple table and does not copy colors or charts. The AI summarizer only works in the latest desktop version of Google Chrome, and its summaries can contain mistakes, so check anything important against the original.

How the site is paid for

The tools are free to use, with no account and no watermark. To help cover hosting and the time it takes to maintain the site, we may show ads, such as Google AdSense. You can read how ads and cookies are handled in our Privacy Policy. Ads never see your files, because your files stay in your browser.
