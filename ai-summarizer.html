Frequently Asked Questions

Is this AI PDF summarizer really free?

Yes. It is free, with no signup, no account, and no limit on how many PDFs you summarize.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The text is read from your PDF inside your browser, and the summary is written by the AI model built into Chrome on your own device. Your file and its text are never sent to our servers or to any AI service.

Why does it say I need Google Chrome?

This tool uses Chrome’s built-in AI, called the Summarizer API. It works in the latest desktop version of Google Chrome. It does not work in Safari, Firefox, or on most phones and tablets. Your computer also needs enough free storage and a fairly modern processor or graphics card for the AI model.

What happens the first time I use it?

Chrome may need to download its AI model once. The download can be large, so use a good connection. After that, the model stays on your device and summaries start faster.

Can it summarize scanned PDFs or very long PDFs?

Long text-based PDFs work. The tool splits them into parts, summarizes each part, and then combines the results, so very long files take more time. Scanned PDFs, which are only pictures of pages, have no text to read, so this tool cannot summarize them.

How accurate is the summary?

The on-device AI is small, so it can miss details or make mistakes. It works best with English text. Always check important facts, numbers, and names in the original document.

Can I also work with images or merge PDFs?

Yes. Try our free Image to PDF converter and Merge PDF tool. They also run fully in your browser.
