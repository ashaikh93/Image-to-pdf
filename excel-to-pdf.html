Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Excel to PDF converter really free?

Yes. It is free, with no signup, no watermark, and no limit on how many files you convert.

Is my spreadsheet uploaded to a server?

No. Your file is read and turned into a PDF inside your browser, on your own device. It never leaves your computer or phone, so it is a good choice for private data such as invoices, payroll, or customer lists.

Which file types can I use?

You can use Excel files (.xlsx and .xls) and CSV files (.csv). Password-protected files cannot be read, so remove the password first.

Will my colors, charts, and images be kept?

No. This tool turns the values in your cells into a clean, simple table. Charts, images, cell colors, and merged cells are not copied. The text appears as you see it in the spreadsheet, such as dates and numbers formatted as shown.

What if my sheet has many columns?

Wide sheets are handled in two ways. The text size is made smaller, and if the table is still too wide, the extra columns continue on the following pages. Choosing Landscape usually gives the best result.

Why do some characters look wrong in the PDF?

The PDF uses a standard font that supports English and most European languages. Text in other scripts, such as Chinese, Japanese, Arabic, or Hindi, may not show correctly. Emoji are not supported either.

Can I convert more than one sheet?

Yes. Pick “All sheets” in the Sheet menu and each sheet is added to the PDF, one after another, with its name as a title.

Can I also make PDFs from images or merge PDFs?

Yes. Try our free Image to PDF converter and Merge PDF tool. They also run fully in your browser.
