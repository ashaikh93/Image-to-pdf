    How to Use This PDF Merger

    Joining PDF files takes less than a minute, and you do not need an account or any software. Below is a full walkthrough, followed by real examples of when it helps.

    Step-by-step tutorial

    1. Add your PDFs. Click “Select PDF files,” or drag two or more PDFs into the dashed box. On a phone, tap the button and choose files from your Files app or Downloads folder. You can add files in several rounds, so it is fine to add a few now and more later. Files that are not PDFs are ignored.
    2. Check the list. Each PDF appears as a row with its name and file size. This makes it easy to spot a wrong file or a duplicate before you merge.
    3. Set the order. Drag a row up or down, or use the ▲ and ▼ buttons, which work well on touch screens. The file at the top becomes the first pages of your merged PDF, and the file at the bottom becomes the last. When you pick many files at once, your device may sort them by name, so always check the order on screen.
    4. Remove mistakes. Click the red × to take a file out of the list. Your original file is never changed or deleted.
    5. Click “Merge PDFs.” The button turns on once you have added at least two files. A spinner shows while the files are combined.
    6. Download and check. Your new file downloads on its own as merged.pdf. Open it, scroll through, and make sure every page is in the right place. Then rename it to something clear, such as “invoices-march.pdf.”

    Every page of every file is copied in order, so nothing is cut out. The tool joins whole files only. If you need just a few pages from a long PDF, trim it first in another app. If the merged file is too big to email, see our guide on how to reduce PDF file size. If your pages are photos, turn them into a PDF first with our Image to PDF converter, and then merge them here.

    Why Use This Tool? Privacy Matters

    The problem with most online PDF mergers

    Many free PDF websites work by uploading your files to their servers. The merge happens there, and you download the result. This is easy, but you cannot see where your files travel, who can access them, or how long they are stored. Invoices contain bank details. Contracts contain names, addresses, and signatures. A company’s files can contain things that should never leave the building. Handing these to a random website is a real risk, and it can also break privacy rules at your workplace or school.

    Your PDFs never leave your device

    This tool works differently. When you add a file, your browser reads it from your own computer or phone. The files are joined right there on the page, using an open-source library called pdf-lib, and the new PDF is saved straight to your Downloads folder. Nothing is sent to our servers, so we never see your documents and cannot lose, leak, or sell them.

    You do not have to take our word for it. Open your browser’s developer tools, go to the Network tab, and merge some files. You will see the page’s code load, but you will not see your PDFs being sent anywhere. One small tip: your merged file is saved on your device, so if you use a shared computer, delete it from your Downloads folder when you are done.

    Simple, fast, and free

    • No account and no installs. It works in any modern browser on Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone, and Android.
    • No watermark and no daily limit. Your merged PDF is clean, and the only limit is your device’s memory.
    • Fast results. Because your files are not uploaded and downloaded, the speed depends on your own device and not on your internet connection.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is this tool to merge PDF files really free?

    Yes. It is completely free, with no signup, no watermark, and no limit on how many times you use it. You can merge as many sets of PDFs as you like.

    Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

    No. Your files are merged inside your own browser and never leave your computer or phone. This makes the tool a safe choice for private papers such as contracts, invoices, IDs, and financial records.

    How many PDFs can I merge at once, and is there a size limit?

    There is no fixed limit on the number of files or their size. The real limit is your device’s memory, because the work is done on your own computer or phone. If a very large set is slow, merge it in smaller groups, and then merge those results together.

    Will merging lower the quality of my PDFs?

    No. The pages are copied from your original files into the new one, so text, images, and layout stay the same. Your original files are not changed. Bookmarks and some interactive features, such as fillable form fields, may not carry over to the merged file.

    Why did my PDF fail to merge?

    The most common reasons are that a file is damaged or protected with a password. The tool shows you the name of the file it could not read. Remove the password in the app that created the PDF, or save a fresh copy of the file, and then try again.

    Can I choose specific pages from a PDF?

    Not in this tool. It joins whole files in the order you set. To use only some pages, first save those pages as a separate PDF in a PDF viewer or editor, and then add that smaller file here.
