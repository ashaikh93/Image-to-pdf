    How to Merge PDF Files

    1. 1

      Add your PDFs

      Click “Select PDF files” or drag two or more PDFs into the box.

    2. 2

      Put them in order

      Drag files up or down. The top file becomes the first pages of your merged PDF.

    3. 3

      Click Merge PDFs

      Your files are combined right in your browser in a few seconds.

    4. 4

      Download

      The merged PDF downloads on its own. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is this tool to merge PDF files really free?

    Yes. It is free, with no signup, no watermark, and no limit on how many times you use it.

    Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

    No. Your files are merged inside your browser, on your own device. They never leave your computer or phone, which makes this a good choice for contracts, IDs, and other private papers.

    How many PDFs can I merge at once?

    There is no set limit. The real limit is your device’s memory. Very large files, or hundreds of them, can be slow on older phones and computers. If that happens, merge them in smaller groups.

    Will merging lower the quality of my PDFs?

    No. Pages are copied from your original files into the new one, so text, images, and layout stay the same. Your original files are not changed.

    Why did my PDF fail to merge?

    The file may be damaged or protected with a password. Remove the password in the app you used to create it, then try again.

    Can I turn images into a PDF too?

    Yes. Use our free Image to PDF converter to turn JPG, PNG, and WEBP images into a PDF, then merge it here with your other files.
