    How to Merge PDF Files

    Why combine PDFs into one file?

    Sending five separate PDFs is messy. They can arrive in the wrong order, one can get left out, and some websites let you upload only one file. A single merged PDF is easier to send, easier to print, and easier for the other person to read from start to finish.

    Common ways people use it

    • Travel documents: Combine flight tickets, hotel bookings, and insurance papers into one file you can open offline.
    • Job applications: Join your cover letter, resume, and certificates into a single PDF that follows the order the employer asked for.
    • Contracts and forms: Put a signed page, the main agreement, and any extra pages together before you send them.
    • Invoices and receipts: Gather a month of paperwork into one file for your accountant or an expense claim.
    • School work: Merge separate assignment parts or scanned pages into one file for your teacher.

    Step-by-step instructions

    1. Add your PDFs. Click “Select PDF files,” or drag two or more PDFs into the dashed box. You can add more files at any time.
    2. Check the list. Each file shows its name and size, so you can be sure you picked the right ones.
    3. Set the order. Drag a file up or down. On a phone, use the ▲ and ▼ buttons instead. The file at the top becomes the first pages of your new PDF.
    4. Remove mistakes. Click the red × to take a file out of the list.
    5. Click “Merge PDFs.” The button turns on once you have at least two files. A spinner shows while your files are combined.
    6. Download and check. Your new file, called merged.pdf, downloads on its own. Open it and scroll through to make sure every page is in the right place.

    All the pages of every file are copied in order, so nothing is cut out. The tool joins whole files only. If you need just a few pages from a long PDF, trim it in another app first.     Frequently Asked Questions

    Is this tool to merge PDF files really free?

    Yes. It is completely free, with no signup, no watermark, and no limit on how many times you use it. You can merge as many sets of PDFs as you like.

    Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

    No. Your files are merged inside your own browser and never leave your computer or phone. That makes this a safe choice for private papers such as contracts, IDs, and financial records.

    How many PDFs can I merge at once?

    There is no fixed limit on the number of files or their size. The real limit is your device’s memory, because the work is done on your own computer or phone. If a very large set is slow, merge it in smaller groups and then merge the results.

    Will the quality of my PDFs get worse?

    No. The pages are copied from your original files into the new one, so text, images, and layout stay the same. Your original files are not changed. Bookmarks and some interactive features, such as fillable form fields, may not carry over to the merged file.

    Why did my PDF fail to merge?

    The most common reasons are that a file is damaged or protected with a password. The tool tells you the name of the file it could not read. Remove the password in the app that created the PDF, or save a fresh copy, and then try again.

    Does it work on iPhone and Android?

    Yes. Open the page in Safari or Chrome, tap “Select PDF files,” and choose your PDFs. Use the ▲ and ▼ buttons to change the order, since dragging can be hard on a touch screen. The merged file will be in your Downloads folder or the Files app.
