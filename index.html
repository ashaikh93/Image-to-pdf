Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image to PDF converter really free?

Yes. It’s free to use with no signup, no watermark, and no limit on how many PDFs you create.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens inside your browser, so your images never leave your device. That makes it a good choice for private documents like IDs, receipts, and contracts.

Which image formats can I convert to PDF?

You can use JPG (JPEG), PNG, and WEBP images. You can mix different formats in the same PDF.

Can I combine multiple images into one PDF?

Yes. Each image becomes its own page in a single PDF, in the order shown in the thumbnails. Drag them to rearrange before converting.

What does “Fit to image” page size mean?

Each page is sized to match its image, so nothing is shrunk or surrounded by blank space. Choose A4 or Letter instead if you plan to print the PDF on standard paper.
