How to Scan Documents on iPhone and Save as PDF (Free, 2026)

6 min read

You need to send a signed contract. Or a copy of your ID for a bank form. Or a stack of receipts for work, or your resume and certificates for a job application. In all of these cases, someone asks for a "scan," and you do not have a scanner at home.

The good news is that your iPhone is already a great scanner. It also saves files as PDF, which is the standard format for documents. A PDF looks the same on every phone and computer, it is easy to email, and most websites accept it. In this guide, you will learn three free ways to scan documents on iPhone and save them as a PDF. No extra app is needed.

Method 1: Use the Built-in Notes App

The Notes app has a document scanner inside it. It finds the edges of the page, straightens it, and cleans up the picture. This is the most popular way to do an iPhone scan to PDF.

  1. Open the Notes app and start a new note.
  2. Tap the camera icon, then tap Scan Documents.
  3. Hold your phone over the page. The scan can happen on its own, or you can tap the round button.
  4. Drag the corners so they match the edges of the page, then tap Keep Scan.
  5. Have more pages? Keep scanning. When you are done, tap Save.
  6. Tap the scan inside your note to open it. Then tap the Share button and choose Save to Files.

Your scan is now saved as a PDF in the Files app. You can also share it right from that menu to Mail or Messages.

Tip: Before you save, tap the filter button to pick Color, Grayscale, or Black & White. Black & White is best for pages with only text.

Method 2: Use the Files App

You can also scan straight into the Files app. This is a good choice if you want the PDF saved in a certain folder right away, without making a note first.

  1. Open the Files app and go to the folder where you want the PDF.
  2. Tap the three dots button at the top of the screen.
  3. Tap Scan Documents.
  4. Hold your phone over the page and scan it, just like in the Notes app.
  5. Adjust the corners, tap Keep Scan, scan any other pages, and tap Save.

The scan is saved as a PDF in that folder. You can tap and hold the file to rename it. Menu names can look a little different on some versions of iOS, but the steps are very close.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free to scan documents on iPhone?

Yes. The Notes and Files scanners are built into your iPhone and cost nothing. Our online tool is free too, with no signup and no watermark.

How can I get the best scan quality?

Use good, even light and place the page on a flat, dark surface. Hold your phone steady and straight above the page, so the edges are not tilted. Avoid shadows from your hand or phone. In the scanner, use Black & White for text pages and Color for photos or stamps.

Is it private?

Scans in Notes and Files stay on your device. If you use iCloud, they may also sync to your iCloud account. Our online tool never uploads your photos, because it works fully inside your browser. For very private papers, delete the files from your phone after you send them.

Can I combine multiple scans into one PDF?

Yes. In Notes and Files, keep scanning pages before you tap Save, and they all go into one PDF. If you already have separate photos, add them all to our tool, put them in order, and convert them to one PDF. Please note that our tool accepts images (JPG, PNG, and WEBP), not PDF files.
