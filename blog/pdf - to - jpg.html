How to Convert PDF to JPG (Free, 2025) — 4 Easy Methods

6 min read

Sometimes a PDF is not the right file type. You may want to share one page in a chat, put a page in a presentation, or post it on social media. Some websites also accept only images, so a PDF will not upload. In all of these cases, you need to convert PDF to JPG.

In this guide, you will learn four free ways to convert PDF pages to images. We cover Windows, Mac, phones, and online tools. Each one uses simple steps, and none of them need an account.

Method 1: Convert PDF to JPG on Windows (Built-in)

Windows does not have a one-click "export to JPG" button for PDFs. But you can use the built-in Snipping Tool to capture a page and save it as a JPG. This works well for one page at a time.

  1. Open your PDF in Microsoft Edge. Right-click the file, choose Open with, and pick Edge.
  2. Zoom in or out so the whole page you want fits on the screen.
  3. Press Windows key + Shift + S. Drag a box around the page.
  4. Click the small pop-up in the corner to open the picture in Snipping Tool.
  5. Press Ctrl + S. Under "Save as type," choose JPEG, name the file, and click Save.

Tip: The sharpness of the image depends on your screen. For the clearest text, zoom in so the page fills as much of the screen as possible.

Method 2: Convert PDF to JPG on Mac (Preview)

Mac has a proper export option inside Preview, so the quality is good.

  1. Open your PDF in Preview. This is the default app for PDFs on most Macs.
  2. Go to the page you want to save.
  3. Click File, then Export.
  4. Next to Format, choose JPEG.
  5. Move the Quality slider to the right for a sharper image. A higher Resolution number also helps with small text.
  6. Pick a name and folder, then click Save.

Preview saves the page you are viewing, so repeat these steps for each page you need.

Method 3: Convert PDF to JPG on iPhone or Android

On a phone, the quickest way is a screenshot. It is not a true file conversion, but it gives you an image you can share right away.

  1. Open the PDF on your phone. Use Files on iPhone, or Google Drive or Files on Android.
  2. Scroll to the page you want and zoom so it fills the screen.
  3. Take a screenshot. On iPhone with Face ID, press the side button and Volume Up together. On most Android phones, press Power and Volume Down.
  4. Open the screenshot in your photo app and crop away anything that is not part of the page.

Keep in mind that iPhone screenshots are saved as PNG files, not JPG. PNG works fine for sharing and posting, and the quality is just as good. Some Android phones save screenshots as JPG.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free to convert PDF to JPG?

Yes. The Windows, Mac, and phone methods in this guide are all free and need no extra software. Many online converters are free too, though some limit how much you can do each day.

How many pages can I convert at once?

With the built-in methods, you convert one page at a time. Online tools often let you convert a whole PDF at once, but free versions may set a limit on pages or file size. For a long PDF, you may need to split the work into smaller parts.

Is it private?

The built-in methods keep everything on your own device, so they are the safest choice. With an online converter, check whether your file is uploaded to a server. For private documents, use Windows, Mac, or your phone instead. Our Image to PDF tool never uploads your files.

Which format is better, JPG or PNG?

JPG files are smaller and work well for photos and most pages. PNG files are larger, but text and sharp lines stay crisper. If your page is mostly text, PNG may look better. If you need a small file to upload or email, choose JPG.
