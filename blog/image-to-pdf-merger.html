Why Use an Image to PDF Merger?

Pictures are great for looking at, but a PDF is better for sharing. Here is why people choose to merge images into one PDF:

  • One file, fixed order. Your pages stay in the order you choose. The person reading them does not have to guess what comes first.
  • Works everywhere. A PDF looks the same on every phone, tablet, and computer. Almost every website and email app accepts it.
  • Easy to send. Instead of attaching ten images, you attach one file. This also avoids the problem of an email service dropping a picture.
  • Ready to print. Each image sits on a proper page, so printing is simple. You can pick A4 or Letter paper and add margins.
  • Looks professional. A clean PDF looks better than a list of camera photos, which matters for job applications and official forms.

Privacy matters too. Many online tools upload your pictures to a server before they make the PDF. That is a risk for IDs, bank papers, and contracts. A tool that works inside your browser keeps your images on your own device. That is how our tool works, and it is why we built it that way.

How to Combine Images to PDF on iPhone and Android

You do not need a computer. The same tool works in the browser on your phone, so you can merge photos right after you take them.

On iPhone

  1. Open pdfimageconverter.online in Safari.
  2. Tap Select images and choose your photos from the photo library.
  3. Dragging can be hard on a small screen, so use the small ◀ and ▶ buttons under each picture to change the order.
  4. Pick your page settings and tap Convert to PDF.
  5. Open the Files app and look in the Downloads folder, or tap the download arrow in Safari.

If your iPhone saves photos as HEIC, go to Settings > Camera > Formats and choose Most Compatible. New photos will then be saved as JPG, which our tool can read. For other iPhone options, see our guide to converting JPG to PDF on iPhone.

On Android

  1. Open the site in Chrome.
  2. Tap Select images and pick your pictures from the gallery or your files.
  3. Use the ◀ and ▶ buttons to arrange the pages.
  4. Tap Convert to PDF. Your PDF will be in your Downloads folder, and you can open it from the Files app.
