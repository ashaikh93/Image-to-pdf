How to Merge PDF Files on iPhone and Android (Free, 2026)

6 min read

You booked a trip, and now you have a flight ticket, a hotel booking, and a car rental, each in its own PDF. Or you have ten receipts to send for a refund. Or your child has three school assignments to turn in as one file. In all of these cases, it is easier to send one PDF than many.

Most people do not have a laptop with them every time. The good news is that you can merge a PDF on iPhone or Android, right from your phone, and it does not have to cost anything. This guide shows you three free ways.

Method 1: How to Merge PDFs on iPhone (Files App and Shortcuts)

The iPhone does not have a big "merge" button, but it has a built-in app called Shortcuts that can combine PDFs. You set it up one time, and then you can use it from the Files app whenever you want.

Set it up once:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap the + button to make a new shortcut.
  2. Name it "Merge PDF."
  3. Open the shortcut's settings and turn on Show in Share Sheet. Choose PDFs as the type of file it accepts.
  4. Add the action Make PDF, and make sure it uses the Shortcut Input.
  5. Add the action Save File and turn on Ask Where to Save.

Use it each time:

  1. Open the Files app and go to your PDFs.
  2. Tap Select and tap each PDF you want to combine.
  3. Tap the Share button and choose your Merge PDF shortcut.
  4. Pick a folder and save your new PDF.

Good to know: Some guides say you can select PDFs in Files, touch and hold, and tap Create PDF. That option usually shows up for images, so if you do not see it for PDFs, use the Shortcuts way. Also, check the page order in your new file. If it is wrong, run the shortcut again and select the files in the order you want.

Method 2: How to Merge PDFs on Android

Android phones do not have a built-in tool to merge PDFs. The Google Files app and Google Drive can open and share PDFs, but they cannot combine them. So you have two choices: use an app from the Google Play Store, or use an online tool (Method 3).

If you want an app, the steps are almost always the same:

  1. Open the Google Play Store and search for "merge PDF."
  2. Pick an app that has good reviews and says it is free. Read the description first.
  3. Open the app and tap Merge or Combine.
  4. Choose your PDFs from your phone, and drag them into the order you want.
  5. Tap Merge and save the new file.

Be careful with free apps. Some show a lot of ads, add a watermark to your pages, or ask you to pay before you can save. Some also upload your files to a server. If your PDFs have private information, such as an ID or a contract, that is a risk. You can also check what permissions the app asks for, and skip any that ask for more than they need.

No. When PDFs are merged, the pages are copied into the new file. Text, images, and layout stay the same. Your original files are not changed.

Why did my PDF not merge?

The most common reasons are that the file is damaged or protected with a password. Remove the password first, then try again. Very large files can also be slow on older phones, so try merging them in smaller groups.
