How to Convert JPG to PDF on iPhone (Free Method, 2025)

Need to turn a photo into a PDF on your iPhone? Maybe a form, a receipt, or a school assignment only accepts PDF files. The good news is that JPG to PDF on iPhone is free, and you do not need to install anything.

In this guide, you will learn three simple ways to convert JPG to PDF on iPhone. Two use tools already built into your phone. The third uses our free online tool, which can put many photos into one PDF in a few taps. Pick the method that fits you best.

Method 1: Using Safari

Safari can save what is on your screen as a PDF. This works best when you have one image to convert and it opens in Safari, such as a picture on a website or in your online storage.

  1. Open the JPG image in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Tap Options at the top of the share sheet.
  4. Choose PDF, then tap Done.
  5. Tap Save to Files, pick a folder, and tap Save.

Good to know: Menu names can change a little between iOS versions. Also, photos that live only in your Photos app will not open in Safari. For those, use Method 2 or Method 3.

Method 2: Using the Files App

The Files app can turn saved photos into a PDF. It is built into every iPhone, so it is a quick way to do an iPhone JPG to PDF free of charge.

  1. Open the Photos app and tap the picture you want.
  2. Tap Share, then Save to Files, and choose a folder.
  3. Open the Files app and go to that folder.
  4. Touch and hold the image, then tap Quick Actions and choose Create PDF.
  5. A new PDF appears next to your image. Touch and hold it to rename or share it.

To use several photos, save them all to the same folder first. Then tap Select, pick the images, tap the More (three dots) button, and choose Create PDF.

This method is simple, but it gives you few choices. You cannot easily set the page size, the margins, or the page order.
